Abstract

This work addresses the task of separating a target speaker’s voice from mixtures using visual cues. While existing discrim- inative approaches achieve strong speech intelligibility, gener- ative diffusion-based methods improve perceptual quality but require many iterative refinement steps. To address these limi- tations, we propose AV-FlowSep, an audio-visual target speaker separation based on conditional flow matching to learn direct transport paths from mixture to clean mel-spectrogram. We adopt a latent Diffusion Transformer where temporal visual cues from the target speaker are integrated via cross-attention. We demonstrate that AV-FlowSep can generate high perceptual quality with as few as a single inference step. Experiments on VoxCeleb2 and LRS2 across both speech-speech and speech- noise scenarios, show that our approach achieves competitive or superior performance compared to existing baselines, with strong cross-dataset generalization and practical computational efficiency.
